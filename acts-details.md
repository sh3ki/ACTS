Our History
Discover how ACTS Computer College started and the journey that shaped us into the institution we are today.

Pioneering Computer Education Since 1987
Established in Sta. Cruz, Laguna, ACTS Computer College began as the town’s first computer school, offering short courses in programming and office applications. By 1990, it expanded into two- and three-year tech-focused programs, laying the foundation for future college degrees.
Growth, Recognition, and Academic Expansion
In 1994, ACTS moved to a larger campus and was officially renamed ACTS Computer College. That same year, CHED approved full degree programs like BSCS, BSIT, BSBA, and BSIM, marking a major leap toward becoming a recognized higher education institution in technology and business.
Scholarship Programs and National Impact
From the Study Now Pay Later Plan in 1996 to the ASAP Scholarship in 2006, ACTS has provided educational opportunities to underprivileged students. With campuses in Laguna and Quezon, ACTS continues its mission to empower lives through quality, affordable education rooted in faith and service.

Senior High Programs
At ACT Computer College, our Senior High School program is built to empower students with the skills and knowledge they need for higher education and career success. We offer specialized strands including ABM, ICT, STEM, and HUMSS — each designed to match students’ unique interests and strengths. With hands-on learning, dedicated instructors, and a future-focused curriculum, we help students unlock their full potential and take confident steps toward their chosen paths.

Tech-Pro Track
Explore innovation, digital systems, and the future of technology. The TechPro track is ideal for students pursuing careers in software engineering, data analysis, cybersecurity, or IT management.
ICT SUPPORT
• Hardware & Software Installation • Basic Network Management
• Troubleshooting & IT Help Desk
COMPUTER PROGRAMMING
• Introduction to Logic & Algorithms • Core Programming Languages (e.g., Python, Java)
• Fundamentals of Web & App Development

Academic Track
Build a strong foundation for your college journey. The Academic track equips students with essential knowledge for university degrees in sciences, business, and humanities
• Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM)
• Business and Entrepreneurship
• Arts, Social Sciences, and Humanities


College Programs
ACTS Computer College offers a dynamic and industry-aligned curriculum designed to equip students with essential skills in technology and innovation. Our programs are tailored to prepare graduates for real-world success in the fast-evolving digital landscape.
BS in COMPUTER SCIENCE (BSCS)
Master algorithms, computing theory, and software development
The BSCS program focuses on computing theories, algorithm design, and developing complex software systems.
BS in INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (BSIT)
Build, manage, and maintain IT systems and infrastructure
The BSIT program focuses on the practical application of IT systems to meet organizational needs.
BS TECHNICAL-VOCATIONAL TEACHER EDUCATION
Train to teach technical and livelihood skills with confidence
The BTVTEd program prepares future teachers to educate students in technical and vocational fields effectively.
BS in BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION (BSBA)
Lead and manage people with strategic HR skills
The BSBA-HRM program prepares students to manage human resources in diverse organizational settings.
BS in ACCOUNTING INFORMATION SYSTEM (BSAIS)
Blend accounting expertise with information technology
The BSAIS program trains students in accounting, business, and computer systems for a tech-driven finance career.
BS in ENTREPRENEURSHIP (BSEntrep)
Learn to build, lead, and grow your own business
The BSEntrep program empowers students with the skills to start, run, and sustain innovative enterprises.
BS in OFFICE ADMINISTRATION (BSOA)
Master office operations and business communication
The BSOA program prepares students for administrative roles in legal, medical, and corporate settings.
